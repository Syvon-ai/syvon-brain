# Syvon Brain

Brain is the persistent creative and work organism. Studio directs work within it; Surface offers ways to obtain creative results from it.

## Language

**Input**: Material selected to inform a piece of work, such as facts, assets, references or prior work. Its role depends on the intention; arrival alone does not authorize production.
_Avoid_: Income, unless discussing actual revenue.

**Intent**: What a person wants to accomplish and why it matters.

**Scope of work**: The bounded agreement connecting an intent to selected inputs, desired outcomes, deliverables and constraints.
_Avoid_: Session scope, which identifies a conversation's home.

**Deliverable**: An artifact produced for the work, such as a deck, video or post.
_Avoid_: Outcome, when only the artifact's existence is known.

**Desired outcome**: The useful change the work is meant to create, together with what would count as evidence.

**Outcome observation**: An attributed, evidence-bearing account of whether a desired outcome was met, not met or remains unmeasured.
_Avoid_: Card outcome, which reports success or failure of a worker's attempt.

**Scope grant**: A person's bounded permission to continue one specific scope of work. It can be paused, revoked or expire.
_Avoid_: File approval, which approves material for use rather than granting continued work.

**Dream Mode**: Continued useful progress on an approved intention within its agreed scope. It may finish without doing anything when no useful work remains.

**Dream receipt**: An account of a bounded continuation attempt, including its reasons, resource use and references to resulting work or needed review.

**Review card**: A person's judgement or correction attached to existing work.

**Ticket**: A person's instruction assigned to an agent; it shares the review-card system rather than creating a separate task system.

**Content**: Shared semantic material from which work is composed.

**Knowledge**: Guidance and understanding carried forward to inform future work; its scope and evidence still matter.
